Measurement: duo. Every sample ran two independent copies of the contender at once, on two threads over two inputs of the size, released together, timed to the later finish, per byte of one copy. Pass --solo to also take a solo sample beside each duo sample.
Use cases: one message per call, at twenty-four input sizes from 64 B to 128 MiB; and many messages per call, a batch of 64-byte messages at twenty-four batch sizes from 1 to 262144. Samples: 96 rounds. A cell whose single hash takes 4 ms or more is sampled in every 4th round, and in every 2nd while the 95% interval of its median is wider than 2% of it; a third row gives the sample counts, in brackets, wherever a cell took fewer.
